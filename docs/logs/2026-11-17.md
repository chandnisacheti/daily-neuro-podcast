## Fact-check fixes
- Story 1: Cut unverified claims that Cogitat is an "Imperial College London spin-out", builds "EEG foundation models", and claimed its models "generalize across different EEG headsets without bespoke calibration", as well as Indivi tracking "drug responses"; rephrased to strictly reflect verified reporting that Indivi acquired Cogitat to advance AI-powered brain monitoring.
- Story 2: Corrected factual claims asserting Remedy Robotics specifically builds remote stroke telesurgery systems preparing for "upcoming human trials" (the source only reports a $62M Series B funding round); reframed the stroke telesurgery discussion as general industry engineering context and noted that Remedy's specific pipeline and milestones were unconfirmed.
- Story 3: Replaced claims that the speech BCI functioned "without daily supervised retraining" with the verified finding that it maintained stable offline syllable decoding across 25 months despite an active spectral signal-to-noise ratio decline.
- Story 4: Changed "patch worn on the head that delivers transcranial ultrasound while simultaneously recording electroencephalography" and "piezoelectric ultrasound transducers" to match the article's wording ("worn on the skin", "delivers gentle ultrasound to deep brain regions", "monitoring brain activity with real-time electrodes").
- Story 5: Cut guessed product form "screenless wearable" and unsupported label "New York digital health unicorn Noom" (the article never states the device is screenless or calls Noom a New York unicorn).
- Story 5: Corrected Nora's claim that Noom Band is included at no upfront cost for members on GLP-1 medications; the article states it will be included for all Noom Health employer and health plan clients, with no upfront cost specifically to the payer.
- Story 5: Added the omitted fact that the 34% weight loss statistic applied specifically to members taking GLP-1 medications.
- Story 5: Clarified that Sensor Bio's 15,000 samples/sec raw cloud pipeline represents Sensor Bio platform claims, which Noom has not confirmed for Noom Band.
