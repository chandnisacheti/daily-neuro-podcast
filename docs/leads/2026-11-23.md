- **Oyi Technology**, funding: Shanghai Aoyi Information Technology Co., Ltd. (OYi Technology) announced the completion of a D-round financing of several hundred million yuan. As a BME MS student focused on biosignal-ML and neuromodulation, OYi's work bridging brain-computer interfaces, electromyography, and embodied neural interfaces offers direct exposure to neural signal decoding and hardware-software integration. - **Burl Concepts**, fda: San Diego-based Burl Concepts received 510(k) clearance (number K260813) for its portable brain ultrasound system, Foqus. This portable brain ultrasound system provides a robust digital signal acquisition platform, which aligns with signal processing and diagnostic instrumentation coursework for a biomedical engineering student. [source](https://www.medicaldaily.com/fda-clears-foqus-ultrasound-pfo-stroke-test-481584)
- **Laronix**, funding: The Brisbane medical technology company has raised AUD $5 million in an oversubscribed pre-Series A round. Laronix applies machine learning and voice AI to reconstruct speech from neural and physiological signals, presenting an exciting opportunity for an MS BME student specialized in biosignal ML. [source](https://itbrief.com.au/story/laronix-raises-aud-5-million-to-expand-voice-restoration)

Consumer health wearables integrating clinical-grade algorithms like sleep apnea detection provide valuable practical insight into physiological signal processing and embedded machine learning. [source](https://news.samsung.com/my/upgraded-sleep-apnea-feature-now-available-on-galaxy-ring-following-regulatory-approvals-from-us-fda-and-korea-mfds)
